# Introduction

Every practice accumulates things it means to keep. The Silver Line asks
one question of that accumulation: what do I keep, and how does it outlive
me? The question has three parts — what is preserved, what is entrusted to
whom, and what is allowed to lapse — and the instrument treats all three as
first-class. A line that could only retain would drift into an immortality
project; the Silver registry carries restraint and lapse families alongside
retention so the drift is refused in the declaration itself.
