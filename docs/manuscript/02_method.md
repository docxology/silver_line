# Method

A succession reading is staged. Intake first: each declared item is
normalized, and malformed content is set aside with a note rather than
crashing or being repaired into a fabricated value. Evaluation second:
keepsakes whose tags intersect the item's tags are matched, and each
matched keepsake's required evidence labels are split into present and
missing. Applicable entrustment requirements also require a nonblank
declared custodian. A lapse-eligible requirement with missing labels reads
NEEDS_PROVISION: eligible but not yet declared, not a retention defect.
Silence is never narrated as a decision.

An explicit release supplies the item's ID, declarer, declaration date and
source reference. When applicable lapse policy permits it, the item reads
DECLARED_RELEASE for non-restraint requirements. Restraint acknowledgements
remain required. I check that the declaration is internally scoped and dated;
I do not verify authority, acceptance or consent. Nothing here authorizes
disclosure, destruction or transfer.

The caller supplies the ISO review date; omitted dates are explicitly UNDATED.
The report binds the subject, item-linked findings and notes, registry content
and version, evaluator version, normalized declaration digest and original
source digest. Accepted, rejected and unmatched counts qualify the scope.
I sort scan sets before emission; required-evidence tuples retain their
declared sequence. Content-addressed reports and snapshots support exact replay.
A duplicate item identity rejects the whole scan before unusable declarations
are discarded. Rejection notes preserve valid supplied IDs, and conflicting
declarations use canonical content to break identity ties before digesting.
